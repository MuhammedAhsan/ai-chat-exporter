## 2. What the Extension is

The Extension reads an AI chat conversation from a page open in your browser and
saves it as a PDF, DOCX, HTML, Markdown, TXT, or JSON file. It runs entirely on
your own machine.

It is provided free of charge. There is no account, no subscription, and no
payment.
